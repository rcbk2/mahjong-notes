`karagiri` is the one coinage here. The Japanese word is neutral about intent
-- a misclick is a 空切り too -- where "fake" is not, but this article is about
the deception, so the loss is the right way round.

**Open question.** 局 is written "hand" throughout, which collides with 手牌.
"4.7 million hands" is normal English mahjong writing and "the hand is ready"
is Book I's own usage, so both readings appear. The alternative is `kyoku` for
局, which removes the collision at the cost of a word the reader must learn.
